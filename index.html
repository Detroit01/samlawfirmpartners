FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I settle legal matters in Indonesia from abroad without traveling? +

You can handle legal cases in Indonesia without traveling through our remote legal representation service. We prepare a Special Power of Attorney from abroad and guide you to legalize Surat Kuasa for Indonesia via Hague Apostille or the Indonesian Embassy/Consulate, enabling our bilingual lawyers to finalize KITAS/KITAP, PT PMA licensing, IPR registration, property deeds, or court representation on your behalf.

What Indonesian legal documents and filings can you finalize for foreign clients from abroad? +

Without you stepping foot in Indonesia, our overseas client legal support covers foreign company establishment (PT PMA & OSS-RBA), expatriate stay and work permits (KITAS/KITAP/RPTKA), Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) registration, land and property legal due diligence and notarial deeds, prenuptial/postnuptial agreements, commercial contract enforcement, and full civil or commercial courtroom advocacy across Indonesia.

When do I need Non-Litigation vs. Litigation services? +

Choose Non-Litigation when drafting contracts, registering IPR, obtaining permits, conducting due diligence, or preventing disputes. Choose Litigation when rights have been violated, a dispute has arisen, or formal legal/court proceedings are involved.

Is the confidentiality of my information and case guaranteed? +

Yes. All client information, documents, and identities are kept strictly confidential from the very first consultation.
