FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreign nationals stay safely outside Indonesia while their legal matters are completed in Indonesia? +

Yes, completely. You do not need to travel to Indonesia or disrupt your safety and schedule abroad. You remain safely in your home country while our advocates execute and finalize your paperwork in Indonesia using a Special Power of Attorney (Surat Kuasa) legalized via Hague Apostille or the Indonesian Embassy/Consulate, covering KITAS/KITAP, PT PMA licensing, IPR registration, property deeds, or court representation until fully completed.

What Indonesian legal documents and filings can you finalize for foreign clients from abroad? +

Without you stepping foot in Indonesia, we handle foreign company establishment (PT PMA & OSS-RBA), expatriate stay and work permits (KITAS/KITAP/RPTKA), Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) registration, land and property legal due diligence and notarial deeds, prenuptial/postnuptial agreements, commercial contract enforcement, and full civil or commercial courtroom advocacy across Indonesia.

When do I need Non-Litigation vs. Litigation services? +

Choose Non-Litigation when drafting contracts, registering IPR, obtaining permits, conducting due diligence, or preventing disputes. Choose Litigation when rights have been violated, a dispute has arisen, or formal legal/court proceedings are involved.

Is the confidentiality of my information and case guaranteed? +

Yes. All client information, documents, and identities are kept strictly confidential from the very first consultation.
